Modern Convenience, Quiet Setting

Suburbia with everyday access built in.

Inwood at Great Notch offers an easy lifestyle with recreational amenities, such as pool membership, tennis and basketball courts and play areas for children, without time-consuming maintenance for busy two-career families.

The location and design of Inwood is unique. Nestled in a park-like setting, within easy reach of most major highways and New York City, it is suburbia in an urban area.
